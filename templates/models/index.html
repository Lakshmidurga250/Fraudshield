Why Accuracy is Insufficient for Financial Fraud Detection

In credit card transaction datasets, fraudulent transactions typically represent fewer than 0.2% of all activity. A naive model that universally predicts "Normal" achieves 99.8% raw accuracy while failing to intercept a single fraudulent event.

Therefore, FraudShield prioritizes PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under Curve), Recall (Sensitivity), and Precision to rigorously measure real detection utility without false sense of security.
